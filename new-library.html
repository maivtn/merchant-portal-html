Quick Questions

How does the partner plan work?
Our partner plan is built on a multi-tier referral system. Partners earn commission based on merchant onboarding and transaction volumes generated through their network.
How do I become an ATM Partner?
To become an ATM Partner, you must register through the ATM Partner portal, complete the KYC verification, and have a designated physical location for cash services.
