VLINKPAY Merchant ATM

Terms & Conditions for Receive / Pay on Behalf

Applies to Merchant ATM and Mobile ATM partners in the VLINKPAY ecosystem.

1. Eligibility
  • KYC / KYB must be completed and approved by VLINKPAY.
  • The partner agreement and operating policy must be accepted.
  • The VLINKPAY account must remain in good standing.
  • A valid bank account or settlement wallet must be available.
2. Service Scope
  • Receive funds from customers based on valid system instructions.
  • Pay out or cash out only after the instruction is confirmed.
  • Support cash-in, cash-out, receive on behalf, and pay on behalf.
  • All actions must be executed through the app, QR, or VLINKPAY system.
3. Operating Rules
  • Every transaction must have a valid order or QR code.
  • Fees must be shown and accepted before confirmation.
  • No off-system transaction is allowed unless approved separately.
  • Transaction status must be updated accurately and on time.
4. Collateral & Limits
  • Partners may need to maintain collateral or a security deposit.
  • Limits are determined by account history and risk profile.
  • Available limit = credit limit - used limit - blocked amount.
  • VLINKPAY may adjust limits when required.
5. Settlement & Payout
  • Transactions are settled on the configured schedule.
  • Recommended payout cycle is T+1 unless configured otherwise.
  • Settlement methods may include bank transfer, wallet balance, or stablecoin.
  • Payout and repayment methods must be configured in advance.
6. Service Fees
  • Fees apply according to the approved configuration, starting from 5% or as agreed.
  • The system must display fee, total amount, and net amount clearly.
  • Partners may only process transactions after agreeing to the fee schedule.
7. Suspension Conditions
  • Exceeded limits or insufficient collateral.
  • Delayed settlement or overdue balance.
  • Invalid KYC / KYB or suspicious information.
  • High dispute rate, fraud risk, or policy violations.
8. Partner Commitment
  • Follow VLINKPAY policies, processes, and operating instructions.
  • Be responsible for confirmed transactions and operational records.
  • Cooperate during audits, checks, and incident reviews.
  • Allow the system to log operational history and evidence.
Recommendation Enable only after approval and collateral confirmation.

Keep receive / pay on behalf active only for approved partners with clear credit and settlement settings.

This page is intended for merchant setup and internal compliance review.
