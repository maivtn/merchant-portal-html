Please review and accept this policy before enabling auto-renewal for your AI Annual package.
What you agree to

Auto-renewal helps keep your AI Annual package active without interruption. Your selected payment method will be charged before the current package expires. You can turn auto-renewal off at any time before the next renewal cycle.

  • The renewal will follow the current AI Annual package term and price at the time of renewal.
  • Please make sure your selected payment method has enough balance and remains valid for future charges.
  • If the payment fails, auto-renewal may not be completed and your package benefits may expire.
  • You can turn auto-renewal off at any time before the next renewal cycle from this screen.
