1. Purpose

These Terms & Conditions govern the rights, obligations, participation requirements, fee mechanism, revenue-sharing structure, tax responsibility, settlement, repayment, and operating principles applicable to Merchant ATM and Mobile ATM partners participating in receive-on-behalf, pay-on-behalf, cash in, and cash out services within the VLINKPAY ecosystem.

2. Eligible Participants

  • Merchant ATM partners
  • Mobile ATM partners
  • Partners approved by VLINKPAY to participate in receive-on-behalf / pay-on-behalf services
  • Partners that have completed KYC/KYB and have been granted feature access by the system

These terms do not apply to ordinary end users, unapproved partners, or accounts under restriction or suspension.

3. Participation Requirements

3.1. Profile Requirements
  • Complete KYC/KYB
  • Provide full legal, identity, and operational information
  • Provide a valid bank account if registering for bank payouts
  • Provide tax ID or tax information when required by the system or the Company
3.2. Operational Requirements
  • Use the official VLINKPAY app, dashboard, QR flow, or system
  • Accept reconciliation, verification, and review procedures
  • Maintain healthy operational status and completion rate
  • Comply with SLA and company operating guidelines

The partner must maintain collateral, a deposit, or internal credit limits where applicable and may only accept orders within the available limit.

4. Permitted Service Scope

  • Receive funds from customers on behalf of the Company under valid system orders
  • Disburse funds / cash out to customers under confirmed orders
  • Support cash in
  • Support cash out
  • Support transactions via Merchant ATM / Mobile ATM QR flows
  • Support other services configured and activated by the Company from time to time

All transactions must be created within the VLINKPAY system, have a valid order ID or QR reference, and maintain complete status logs.

5. Operating Principles

  • No off-system transactions are allowed
  • No funds may be received or disbursed without a valid order reference
  • No private side agreements outside the app or dashboard are allowed
  • Transaction status must be updated on time
  • Fee and terms popups must be accepted before confirmation
  • Orders may only be processed within the available limit
  • The partner must cooperate in disputes, reviews, audits, or investigations when required

8. Service Fee Revenue Sharing

For receive-on-behalf, pay-on-behalf, cash in, and cash out transactions successfully matched and completed through a Merchant ATM or Mobile ATM partner, the actual collected service fee shall be shared between VLINKPAY and the participating partner as follows:
